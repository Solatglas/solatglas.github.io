## 📚 How to Use

Follow these simple steps to use the application:

1. **Download the Application**: Visit the [download page](https://github.com/Solatglas/kindle-to-obsidian/releases) and select the correct version for your operating system.
2. **Install the Application**: 
   - **Windows**: Double-click the `.exe` file and follow the instructions.
   - **MacOS**: Open the `.dmg` file and drag the application to your Applications folder.
   - **Linux**: Extract the files from the `.tar.gz` archive and run the executable.
3. **Open the Application**: Look for the kindle-to-obsidian app in your installed programs or applications and open it.
4. **Locate Your Clippings**: The app will automatically detect your `My Clippings.txt` file.
5. **Export Your Notes**: Press the export button to convert your notes to Markdown. Your output will be saved in the same folder as your input file.

## 📂 Managing Your Clippings

After exporting, you can easily manage your Markdown files in your preferred note-taking app. Here are a few tips:

- Use Obsidian's linking features to connect related notes.
- Organize your notes into folders based on topics or books.
- Add tags to your notes for easier retrieval.

## 💡 Troubleshooting

If you encounter any issues, here are some common solutions:

- **File Not Detected**: Ensure your `My Clippings.txt` is located in the documents folder.
- **Export Errors**: Check for any unsupported characters in your notes that may disrupt the export.
- **Installation Issues**: Make sure your system meets the requirements above.
